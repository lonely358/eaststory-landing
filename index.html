About the Product

EastStory Vault is a digital collection of long-form narrative stories from Chinese history, researched and written by an independent creator. Each story is told in a vivid, story-first style while keeping facts and legends clearly separated, with sources noted for every major claim.

The collection focuses on the stories that rarely make it into textbooks: untold moments around famous leaders, palace intrigues of imperial China, remarkable criminal cases, succession struggles, and small people who changed the course of history.
